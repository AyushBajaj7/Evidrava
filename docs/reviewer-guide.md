# Evidrava reviewer guide

This guide prepares a future independent reviewer for the feasibility pilot. It is not a review record, does not name a reviewer, and does not freeze the protocol. The workbook and proposal remain DRAFT until the research team and supervisor make those decisions.

## Why a second person matters

The registry may later support statements about what was publicly known at a historical date. An extractor can make a good-faith mistake about the order, page, matter, value or date. A reviewer must be a real person distinct from the extractor who checks the original source and records their own name and date. Automated extraction, this guide and an AI assistant cannot serve as that second reviewer.

## Before a review session

1. Confirm the reviewer is distinct from the person who entered the claim.
2. Use a study copy of the registry; keep the received v3.3 workbook unchanged.
3. Open the saved original PDF, not only machine-extracted text. The text is a navigation aid and can omit tables or layout.
4. Check the intended matter, episode and label version. Do not assume a shared company name means two proceedings are the same matter.
5. Leave public-date verification, first disclosure and legal history unresolved when the original evidence does not establish them.

## Check every required claim

For each assigned claim, the reviewer should independently confirm:

- the cited order belongs to the stated matter;
- the cited PDF page exists and contains the relevant passage;
- the passage supports the exact recorded value, not just a related allegation;
- the episode, instrument and conduct interval match the intended record;
- the source has a documented publication-date basis before it is used in a historical snapshot; and
- the reviewer identity and review date are recorded separately from the extractor.

If any part is unsupported, record the appropriate unresolved or invalid outcome rather than forcing a value. A completed unresolved review can document the work, but it is not valid audited evidence.

## Suggested first-five-matter routine

1. The extractor enters a provisional claim with order, page and locator.
2. The reviewer opens the original PDF and checks the claim against the source.
3. The two people compare disagreements and record an adjudication path if required.
4. Record real extraction, review and adjudication time separately.
5. Re-run the workbook checker and preserve its report. The checker reports structural issues; it does not certify that the reviewer is independent or that their interpretation is legally correct.

## What not to conclude

- A download timestamp, listing date or document signature date is not automatically the verified public date.
- A readable passage does not by itself prove the first episode-specific disclosure.
- A valid audit slot does not prove market-data coverage, a complete historical snapshot or detector performance.
- The current 30-record workload exercise is not the future random research sample.

The first review sessions should be used to measure practical workload and uncover ambiguous source patterns. They cannot turn the project into PASS while the protocol is DRAFT, the frame is unfrozen, and the broader feasibility conditions remain open.
