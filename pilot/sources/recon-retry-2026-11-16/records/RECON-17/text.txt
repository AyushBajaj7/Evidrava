SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA 
 
Appeal No. 3155 of 2018 
 
Kunduru Sri Ranga Reddy           :     Appellant 
Vs. 
CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai       :    Respondent 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appellant filed an application dated nil (received on July 10 , 2018) under the Right to 
Information Act, 2005 (“ RTI Act ”). The respondent vide letter dated July 17 , 2018, 
responded to the appellant.  The appellant filed this Appeal dated August 7, 2018 against 
the said response.  I have carefully considered the application, the response and the Appeal 
and find that the matter can be decided based on the material available on record.  
 
2. From the appeal, I note that the appellant is aggrieved by the respon dent’s response to his 
application.  
 
3. Queries in the appellant’s application – The appellant made the following requests. 
 
1. Provide information on trading done on his trading terminal from August 1, 2014 to 
December 31, 2015 and the details of transactions done under his trading certificate 
no. INZ010004612 and arrange to provide complete information report from BSE.  
2. Make a detailed investigation in the matter  of alleged manipulation of his trading 
terminal. 
 
4. Grounds in Appeal – The appellant reiterated the request made in the application. 
 
5. I have perused the response of the respondent in light of the grievance raised in the Appeal 
and my observations are as under –  
 
5.1 In response to  the query no. 1 , the appellant was informed that the information is not 
maintained by SEBI in the normal course of regulation of securities market. In response to 
query no. 2, the respondent observed that the same is in the nature of seeking action and

accordingly the same cannot be construed as information under RTI Act. Without assailing 
any of the said observations, the present appeal is filed wher ein the earlier requests were 
reiterated.  
 
5.2 I do not find any reason to disbelieve the response that the details sought under query no. 1 
are not maintained by SEBI in the normal course of regulation of securities market . In this 
context, I note that the Hon’ble Supreme Court in Central Board of Secondary Education 
& Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors (Judgment dated August 9, 2011) , held that “……… 
But where the information sought is not a part of the record of a public authority, and where such information 
is not required to be maintained under any law or the rules or regulations of the public authority, the Act 
does not cast an obligation upon the public authority, to collect or collate such non- available information and 
then furnish it to an applicant.” In view of these observations, I find that the respondent did not 
have an obligation to obtain the information and furnish to the appellant. 
 
5.3 I agree with the observation of the respondent that query no. 2 is in the nature of seeking 
action and hence cannot be construed as information under RTI Act.  In this context, I note 
that the Hon’ble CIC in Shivanandan Yadav v. CPIO, Damodar Valley Corporation, 
Kolkata, (Decision No. CIC/POWER/A/2017/105911, order dated 01.12.2017) observed 
that RTI Act is not the proper law for redressal of grievances and there are other appropriate 
fora for resolving such matters. 
 
5.4 Further, the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi in th e matter of Ms Vandana Mittal v. CIC and 
Others [ W. P. (C) 7244/2009]  held that if no ground for interference in the impugned 
response is made out in the appeal, such appeal is liable to be rejected. I find that the present 
appeal does not specify any grounds on which the response is assailed and hence the appeal 
is liable to be rejected. 
 
6. In view of these observations, I do not find that any interference with the response of the 
respondent is warranted. The Appeal is accordingly dismissed.
