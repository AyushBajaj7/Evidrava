April 22, 2003

 

INVESTIGATIONS ENFORCEMENT AND SURVEILLANCE

IES – ID4

 

SUB : DSQ BIOTECH LTD.

 

Chairman, SEBI has passed orders dated 10.04.03 u/s 11B of SEBI ACT, 1992 against DSQ Biotech Ltd., its main Promoter Shri Dinesh Dalmia and M/s Pillaiyar Pattiyar Textiles Ltd. ( a group company of DSQ Biotech Ltd.) for their role in the price manipulation of the scrip of DSQ Biotech Ltd. Vide the aforesaid order, Chairman has in exercise of the powers conffered on him under Section 4(3) read with Section 11B of SEBI Act 1992 and Regulation 11 and 12 of SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 1995 ordered the above mentioned entities not to access the capital markets or deal in securities for a period of 5 years with immediate effect.

 

Vide orders dated 08.04.03, Chairman SEBI has also passed orders u/s 11B of SEBI Act against M/s BKG Management Services Ltd., Kolkatta, M/s HemangiKolkatta, M/s Glix Kolkatta, M/s OlypabKolkatta not to deal in securities for a period of 2 years for their involvement in the price manipulation of the scrip of DSQ Biotech Ltd.
