BEFORE THE APPELLATE AUTHORITY 
(Under the Right to Information Act, 2005) 
SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA 
 
Appeal No. 4004 of 2020   
 
Pradeep Kumar 
 
: Appellant 
 Vs.  
CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai : Respondent 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appellant had filed an application dated October 09 , 2 020 (received by SEBI on October 14 , 
2020) under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (“RTI Act ”).  The respondent , by a letter dated  
November 09, 2020, responded to the application filed by the appellant. The appellant filed an appeal 
dated December 03, 2020  (received by the Office of Appellate Authority on December 08, 2020) , 
against the said response dated November 09, 2020. I have carefully considered  the application, the 
response and the appeal and find that the matter can be decided based on the material available on 
record.  
2. Queries in the application – The appellant, vide his application dated October 09, 2020, sought the 
following information pertaining to Rose Valley Real Estates & Constructions Limited: 
1. The Assets Disposal Committee constituted by Hon'ble Kolkata High Court has been mentioned in 
SEBI’s letter dated 16 September 2020. Please provide full address, phone number and email id and  
website of the said committee so that contact can be established. 
2. The applicant is a native of District Rae bareli, Uttar Pradesh and wants complete information 
regarding the process of applying to the Assets Disposal Committee. 
3. The respondent, in response to the application, informed that SEBI is not a party in the Writ Petition 
bearing no. W.P. 275 of 2015 which is pending before the Calcutta High Court (Original Side) and 
hence, SEBI does not have the requested information. Further, it was informed that SEBI does not 
have the information in respect of procedure for making application/ request for refund before the 
Assets Disposal Committee.

4. Grounds of Appeal – The appellant, in his appeal, has stated that the he has been informed regarding 
the unavailability of the requested information with SEBI. The appellant has further submitted that 
the respondent has not transferred the application to the related department under Section 6(3) of the 
RTI Act.  
5. On perusal of the queries, I find that the respondent has adequately addressed them by providing the 
information available with him. Further, I note that the appellant has not made any specific 
submission regarding the response. Accordingly, I do not find any deficiency in the response.  
 
6. With respect to the submission of the appellant regarding obligation of the respondent under section 
6(3) of the RTI Act for transferring the application to the concerned public authority, attention is 
drawn to the matter of Shri Ketan Kantilal  Modi vs. Central Board of Excise & Customs (Decision dated 
September 22, 2009), wherein the Hon’ble CIC had held that “A petitioner is obliged under Section 6(1) to file 
his RTI-application before the CPIO of the public authority which is the ‘concerned p ublic authority’, which holds the 
information within the meaning of Section 2(j) of the Act.. The decision to transfer an RTI application to another public 
authority under Section 6(3) is to be CPIO’s given the circumstances surrounding a particular reques t for information. 
These circumstances may vary from case to case and petition to petition and cannot be predetermined” . In view of 
these observations, I find that an applicant cannot file an application under the RTI Act, before an 
unrelated public authority, with an expectation that the CPIO of such public authority would transfer 
the same, in accordance with section 6(3) of that Act, to the concerned public authority where the 
information may be held. Therefore, in the instant case, no fault can be found with the respondent for 
not transferring the appellant’s application to any other public authority.  
  
7. In view of the above observations, I find that there is no need to interfere with the decision of the 
respondent. The Appeal is accordingly dismissed.
