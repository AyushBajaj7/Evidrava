BEFORE THE APPELLATE AUTHORITY 
(Under the Right to Information Act, 2005) 
SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA 
 
Appeal No. 1706 of 2013 
 
Shantaram Walavalkar              :     Appellant  
Vs. 
CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai    :    Respondent 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appellant had filed an application dated March 15, 2013, under the Right to Information 
Act, 2005 (hereinafter referred to as " RTI Act"). The respondent vide letter dated April 10, 
2013, responded to the appellant. The appellant had filed a first appeal dated April 13, 2013 
(received at SEBI on April 25, 2013) i.e. Appeal No. 1672 of 2013, against the said response.  
 
2. Upon a consideration of the appellant’s abovemen tioned first appeal, this Appellate Authority 
vide Order dated May 15, 2013 (Reference No. AAO/1656/RTI/05/2013) had disposed of 
the same inter alia in respect of the query at para 2(vii) of that Order i.e. “Please inform status and 
action on Appeal dated 12-03-2013 received on 14-03 -2013 related to original complaint dated 30-04-2012 
as above. Please also inform all noting on said appe al by Chairman and/or other employee of SEBI. Please 
also provide copy of reply, if any, to Shantaram Walavalkar.”, with the following direction to the 
respondent –  
 
“to provide the appellant with information relating to  the present status of such complaint alongwith the 
complaint number as generated through SCORES, in accordance with the provisions of the RTI Act read with 
the Right to Information Rules, 2012, within 15 working days from the date of receipt of this order.” 
 
3. I note that in compliance with the abovemen tioned Order of the Appellate Authority, the 
respondent vide letter dated June 3, 2013, had provided the appellant with information in 
respect of the Appeal dated 12-03-2013 received on 14-03-2013, as under –  
 
“Complaint registration no.: SEBIP/MH12/0008085/1  
Present status: Case disposed of.”      
 
4. In light of the above, the appellant has filed th e instant appeal dated June 7, 2013 (received at 
SEBI on June 13, 2013), against the respondent’s response dated June 3, 2013. 
 
5. In the instant appeal, the appellant while referring to the complaint registration no. provided 
through the respondent’s response dated June 3, 2013, has contended that "MH12 means to me

is complaint registration of Year 2 012 and therefore, my letter of year 2013 cannot be uploaded in Year

6. I note that this Appellate Authority vide Or der dated May 15, 2013, had clearly directed the 
respondent “to provide the appellant with information relati ng to the present status of such complaint 
(Appeal dated 12-03-2013 received on 14-03-2013) al ongwith the complaint number as generated through 
SCORES”. Upon a consideration of the respondent’s res ponse dated June 3, 2013, I find that 
the requisite information in respect of the appellant’s query at para 2(vii) of the 
aforementioned Appellate Author ity’s Order i.e. complaint registration no. and its present 
status, has been provided by the respondent. However, such information when viewed in light 
of the appellant's submission at para 5 supra a ppears incomplete. In this context and from the 
records made available to me, I note that the appellant's complaint dated March 12, 2013 was 
uploaded on SCORES on April 5, 2013, as a reminder to the appellant's earlier complaint 
dated October 1, 2012, which had complain t registration no. SEBIP/MH12/0008085/1. In 
view of the factual situation, I find no defici ency with the respondent’s response dated June 3, 
2013. 
 
7. I, therefore, find that there is no need to interfere with the decision of the respondent. The 
appeal is accordingly dismissed.
