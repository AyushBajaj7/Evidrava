CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai       :    Respondent 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appellant filed two applications dated June 24, 2018 through RTI MIS Portal under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005 (“RTI Act”). The respondent vide letter dated July 18, 2018, 
responded to the appellant.  The appellant  filed the present Appeals on August 19, 2018 , 
against the said response. I have carefully considered the application, the response  and the 
Appeal and find that the matter can be decided based on the material available on record.  
 
2. From the Appeals, I note that the appellant is aggrieved by the respondent’s response to his 
applications.  
 
3. Query in the appellant’s application – The appellant stated that documents and original 
bond certificates seeking refund of investments of his mother in the matter of Sahara were 
submitted to SEBI and requested for ‘final action decision report’ and proof of refund.  
 
4. Grounds in Appeal s – The appellant reiterated the request for informatio n contained in 
his applications.  
 
5. I have perused the response of the respondent in light of the grievance raised in the Appeal. 
The respondent informed that the application for refund is under process . Since the 
application is under process, it is evident that proof of refund as sought by the appellant is 
non-existent. Accordingly, the response adequately addressed the query of the appellant and 
I find no reason to interfere with the response.
