Under Regulations 28(2) read with R egulations 38 (2)  of the SEBI (Intermediaries) 
Regulations, 2008 in respect of Khandwala Securities L imited (earlier known as 
Khandwala Finance L imited)(SEBI Registration No. INB 230600030 ) in the matter of 
DSQ Biotech Ltd.  
  
1. Securities and Exchange Board of India ( 'SEBI') conducted investigation into the trading in the 
scrip of M/s DSQ Biotech Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as ‘ DSQ' / 'Company’) for the period 
from December 1999 to January 2001  ('investigation period ')to ascertain any instances of 
irregularity in the preferential allotment of shares by the company, price manipulation in t he 
scrip and subsequent off -loading of the unlisted shares in the securities market by DSQ and 
related entities. The shares of DSQ w ere listed on the Ahmedabad Stock Exchange ("ASE"), 
BSE Ltd (" BSE"), Calcutta Stock Exchange Association Ltd. ("CSE"), Delhi Stock Exchange 
Ltd.("DSE"), Madras Stock Exchange ("MSE")and National Stock Exchange of India Limited 
("NSE") during the relevant time.  
 
2. It was noticed that DSQ had increased the share capital through a preferential issue during the 
investigation period. It was observed that prior to and after the period of the said issue i.e., 
between December 1999 and January 2001 , abnormal trading activity in the scrip was noticed at 
NSE and BSE. The price and the volume in the scrip during the above mentioned period we re 
attributed to the alleged synchronized trades and fictitious trades executed by certain entities 
through several stock brokers.  
 
3. It was observed that M/s Khandwala Securities L imited ('the Noticee'), a stock broker of NSE 
had dealt in the scrip of DSQ for up to 33 clients at NSE during the period December 1999 to 
April 2000 (12 clients) and September 2000 to January 2001 (21 clients). Th e scrip of DSQ was 
available for trading on the weekly account period settlement as well as the rolling settlement 
segments during the investigation period. The total quantity traded during the period December 
01, 1999 to May 02, 2000 on the account segment was 37,62,400 shares. Investigation prime facie

SCHEDULE II- Code Of Conduct For Stock Brokers 
A. GENERAL 
(1) Integrity: A stock-broker, shall maintain high standards of integrity, promptitude and fairness in the

(2) Exercise of Due Skill and Care : A stock -broker, shall act with due skill, care and diligence in

market beyond reasonable levels not commensurate with his financial soundness. 
 (5) Compliance with Statutory Requirements: A stock broker shall abide by all the provisions of

time as may be applicable to him.
